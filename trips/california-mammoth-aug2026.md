## TL;DR

A 6-night Eastern Sierra trip that maxes the natural-beauty side (Carissa's wheelhouse — lakes everywhere, 11k-ft summit, surreal Mono Lake tufas, 4,800-yr-old bristlecone pines), wraps in a 1.5-day Irvine visit with parents on either side, and front-loads enough aesthetic moments (Mobius Arch sunrise, Mono Lake paddle, turquoise alpine lakes) to keep Ashi's Instagram side fed.

Stay 4 nights at **Tamarack Lodge** on Twin Lakes (1924 historic lakeside cabins) or **Sierra Nevada Resort** in Mammoth Village (1967 Hollywood-history character, spa, walkable). Food is meat-and-mountains, not sushi country — get the omakase fix at **Hana re** (Costa Mesa, Michelin) the night before flying inland. **Skadi**, **Convict Lake**, and **Carson Peak Inn** (1966 wood-paneled vintage steakhouse in June Lake) punch above the destination's reputation.

## Locked Day-by-Day

### Day 1 — Sat Aug 1 · Pittsburgh → Irvine
- PIT → SNA midday (1-stop)
- Dinner with parents
- **Optional milestone meal**: Hana re (Costa Mesa) — 1-star Michelin omakase, $200pp, 15-course. Book 2+ weeks ahead via Tock. Handles wife's no-cheese + husband's no-yoghurt easily.

### Day 2 — Sun Aug 2 · Irvine → Mammoth
- Late brunch with parents
- ~Noon: Drive Irvine → Hawthorne Municipal (HHR) ~50 mi / 60-90 min
- Afternoon: **Advanced Air HHR→MMH** (~1.5 hr) — only Sun/Thu/Sat service in summer 2026
- Late afternoon: Arrive Mammoth Yosemite (MMH), pick up rental, check into hotel
- Light dinner: Side Door Wine Bar (wood-paneled, Carissa) or Toomey's (casual)
- Early bed — altitude is 7,900 ft

### Day 3 — Mon Aug 3 · Acclimate (Devils Postpile + Hot Creek)
- AM: **Black Velvet Coffee** (Blue Bottle-tier), gentle walk in Mammoth Lakes Basin (Lake Mary, Twin Lakes)
- Late AM: **Devils Postpile + Rainbow Falls** — mandatory shuttle from Mammoth Mountain Adventure Center. *Be there before 10:30am to avoid 30-45 min queue.* Easy 2-mi RT hike. Bring industrial bug spray.
- Lunch: **Lakefront Restaurant at Tamarack Lodge** — wood-paneled, white tablecloths, lake view, piano weekends
- Late PM: **Hot Creek Geological Site** at golden hour — azure pools, steam vents, no soaking but photogenic
- Dinner: **Toomey's** (no rezzy, comfort food)

### Day 4 — Tue Aug 4 · HERO DAY · Mono Lake + June Lake Loop
- 6:30 AM: Coffee at Stellar Brew, drive to Lee Vining (~45 min north on 395)
- 8:00 AM: **Caldera Kayak Mono Lake South Tufa paddle** — book in advance. *Be off water by noon — winds spike.*
- Lunch: **Whoa Nellie Deli at Tioga Gas Mart** (Lee Vining) — "legendary gas-station fine dining" per NYT. Baja fish tacos w/ wasabi crème fraîche, lobster taquitos, Mono Lake views.
- PM: Drive **June Lake Loop (Hwy 158)** — June/Gull/Silver/Grant lakes. Swim at **June Lake beach** (finest sand + warmest water in the loop)
- Dinner: **Carson Peak Inn** (June Lake) — 1966 vintage steakhouse, dark green vinyl booths, mounted trout, prime rib. *Book ahead: 760-648-7575.*

### Day 5 — Wed Aug 5 · SOUTH DAY · Alabama Hills sunrise + Bristlecones
*Heavy but worth-it day.*

- **4:30 AM** — Coffee, drive Mammoth → Lone Pine (2 hr south on 395)
- **~5:50 AM** — Park near Whitney Portal Rd / Movie Road, walk to **Mobius Arch** with headlamps
- **~6:00 AM sunrise** — Mount Whitney framed inside the natural arch, alpenglow on the Sierra wall
- 7:30-8:30: Movie Road exploration, breakfast in Lone Pine
- 9:30: Drive Lone Pine → Big Pine → up White Mountain Rd to **Schulman Grove** (1 hr 45 min, switchbacks to 10,000 ft)
- 11:30 AM-1:00 PM: **Ancient Bristlecone Pine Forest** — visitor center + **Discovery Trail** (easy 1-mi loop, gentler than Methuselah's 4.5 mi)
- 1:30: Drive down to Bishop (1.5 hr)
- 3:00 PM: Late lunch at **Erick Schat's Bakkery** (Gold Rush-era Sheepherder bread)
- 4:00 PM: Drive Bishop → Mammoth (1 hr)
- 5-7 PM: Recovery time at hotel — spa/pool
- 7:30 PM Dinner: **Skadi** — Nordic chef's counter, 6 tables. *Reserve by text 760-914-0962 NOW.*
- 10 PM optional: Stargazing at **Minaret Vista** (dark sky designated)

*Note: Manzanar NHS is closed Tue-Wed — out for this trip.*

### Day 6 — Thu Aug 6 · Gondola + Fly home
- 7 AM: Coffee + breakfast
- 8 AM: **Mammoth Mountain Gondola** to summit (11,053 ft) — 400-mi visibility. Eleven53 Café for coffee at top, walk upper rim trail 30 min. Pack a layer (15°F+ colder than base).
- 11 AM: Check out, drive to MMH (~20 min)
- Afternoon: **Advanced Air MMH → HHR → PIT**
