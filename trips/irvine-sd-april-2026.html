The Key Tradeoff: $615 vs. Nonstop Comfort

Nonstop AA/UA to LAX ($457/pp) — Carissa's preference. Arrive 8:10pm, at parents' by 9pm. Peace of mind, no connection risk.

The return is a connection regardless — cheapest reasonable options are $248-$290/pp.

Decisions to Make

Two questions drive everything else:

1. Nonstop or earlier arrival?

LAX nonstop (arrive 9pm) vs. SNA connecting (arrive ~3-4pm). On a 3-night trip, those extra hours are valuable. Is Carissa's nonstop preference strong enough for this one?

2. How to split the 4 nights?

2+2 (Layout A) is balanced. 3+1 (Layout B) maximizes parents. 1+3 (Layout C) maximizes SD. Which relationship needs the time more this trip?
