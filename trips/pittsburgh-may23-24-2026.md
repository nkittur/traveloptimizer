## Saturday May 23 — Indoor-leaning plan

### Mid-morning · Phipps "Alice's Adventures in Wonderland" flower show
- **Time**: 9:30 AM – 5 PM
- **Where**: Phipps Conservatory, Oakland
- **Why**: Just opened May 16 — peak bloom. Conservatory architecture itself is photogenic, characters/sets staged as installations. Ashi will photograph everything; Carissa gets covered indoor garden; works as rain shelter.
- **Bonus**: *Discovery Garden Day* 11a-1p (nature crafts, summer kickoff).
- **Source**: [Phipps Conservatory calendar](https://phipps.conservatory.org/calendar)

### Lunch · Strip District Terminal Market
- **Time**: 11 AM – 3 PM
- **Where**: Strip District Terminal
- **Why**: Sat-only food vendor lineup with DJ Breis Gordan, covered space, perfect rain plan. Niki's foodie box checked.
- **Source**: [Pittsburgh Magazine](https://www.pittsburghmagazine.com/things-to-do-this-weekend-in-pittsburgh/)

### Afternoon · 59th Carnegie International — "If the word we"
- **Time**: Museum hours (~10 AM – 5 PM)
- **Where**: Carnegie Museum of Art, Oakland (+ partner installs at Mattress Factory, Kamin Science Center, Children's Museum)
- **Why**: 61-artist contemporary art exhibition that opens once every 4 years. Just opened May 2, runs through Jan 2027 — but the buzz is *now*. Ashi gets aesthetic/design-forward contemporary art; Niki gets the cultural anchor. Optional: drive over to Mattress Factory North Side for the Martínez Garay + Kameya install ("The ash no longer remembers what caused the fire").
- **Source**: [Carnegie International](https://carnegieart.org/exhibition/59th-carnegie-international/), [Visit Pittsburgh insider guide](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/insiders-guide-59th-carnegie-international/)

### Saturday Evening — Carissa's pick
### MCG Jazz · An Evening with Tamara Tunie + jazz ensemble
- **Time**: 6 PM and 8:30 PM sets
- **Where**: MCG Jazz, Strip District
- **Why**: Broadway actress (Law & Order: SVU's M.E. Warner) + jazz quartet at MCG's wood-paneled Strip venue. This is Carissa's exact lane — vintage atmosphere, live jazz, supper-club vibe.
- **Source**: [NEXT Pittsburgh memorial day guide](https://nextpittsburgh.com/events/12-things-to-do-in-pittsburgh-memorial-day-weekend-2026/)

### Alt evening · Hedwig and the Angry Inch
- **Where**: Greer Cabaret Theater, Cultural District
- **Why**: Tony-winning rock musical in mid-run. Cabaret format = atmospheric. Edgier than Tamara Tunie — pick based on Carissa's mood.
- **Source**: [Pittsburgh Magazine](https://www.pittsburghmagazine.com/things-to-do-this-weekend-in-pittsburgh/)

### Alt evening · Skate the Square (closes May 25 — last weekend)
- **Time**: 12 PM – 10 PM (DJs Kristyles + Fuzzy on Sat — "Girly Pop" set)
- **Where**: Market Square, Downtown
- **Why**: Pop-up retro roller rink with cocktail bar + DJ. Final weekend before it closes Memorial Day. Ashi-aesthetic + Carissa-fun + indoor-covered if rain.
- **Source**: [Downtown Pittsburgh](https://downtownpittsburgh.com/skatethesquare/)

## Sunday May 24 — Front-loaded plan (storms after 2 PM)

### Morning — the dry window (before 2 PM)

#### Option A · Shore Thing floating barge brunch
- **Time**: 12 PM – 2 PM (before storms; bar runs til 10 PM but skip post-2 PM)
- **Where**: Allegheny Landing, North Shore (between the Sister Bridges)
- **Why**: Brand-new floating barge that just opened for the season. **La Vispera stained-glass canopy** installed this year — Ashi will photograph it; Niki gets the riverfront. BG Brewing food + drink.
- **Source**: [Riverlife](https://riverlifepgh.org/shore-thing-returns-to-allegheny-river-over-memorial-day-weekend/)

#### Option B · Phipps Hoppy Hour with Rabbit Wranglers
- **Time**: 10:30 AM – 12 PM
- **Where**: Phipps Conservatory
- **Why**: Live rabbits roaming the conservatory. Indoor, covered, weather-proof. Quirky Ashi photo op. Pair with the Alice show if you missed it Saturday.
- **Source**: [Phipps](https://phipps.conservatory.org/calendar)

### Afternoon · Pizza Olympics at Spirit · THE play of the weekend
- **Time**: 2 PM – 8 PM (recommend **arrive 2 PM, plan to be done by 5 PM** — storms roll in)
- **Where**: Spirit, 242 51st St, Lawrenceville
- **Why**: 11 pizzerias compete — Driftwood, Badamo's, Spak, Piccolo Forno, La Grassa, Lupo, Slice on Broadway, Spirit, and more. Rooftop bocce courts open (weather permitting). Espresso by Espresso a Mano. **$32 Pizza Pass** per person, kids under 10 free.
- **Family fit**: Niki's #1 (foodie heaven + variety) / Carissa (atmospheric Lawrenceville + indoor pizza-counter room) / Ashi (urban energy + photogenic pizza counters).
- **Rain plan**: Spirit has a large indoor pizza-counter room and covered patio — Pizza Olympics happens rain or shine. The *rooftop bocce* gets weather-impacted, not the food. Bring a light jacket.
- **Source**: [Spirit calendar](https://www.spiritpgh.com/calendar/2026/5/24/pittsburgh-pizza-olympics-2026), [NEXT Pittsburgh](https://nextpittsburgh.com/events/12-things-to-do-in-pittsburgh-memorial-day-weekend-2026/)

### Evening · indoor pivot (storms peaking)
Pick one:
- **Hidden Harbor** (3422 Forbes Ave, Squirrel Hill) — tiki bar with leather banquettes, dark, no windows. Carissa's atmospheric-bar bullseye.
- **Cure** (5336 Butler St, Lawrenceville) — 3-block walk from Spirit. Higher-end seasonal Italian for a post-pizza wind-down dinner.
- **Tako** (214 6th St, Downtown) — modern Mexican with photogenic plating, indoors.
- **Row House Cinema** (4115 Butler St, Lawrenceville) — also walking distance from Spirit. Check their current series.

### Sunday wash-out backup (if forecast deteriorates to "heavy storms all day")
If Sunday becomes too wet for outdoor at all, the full-indoor pivot:
- AM: **Phipps** Hoppy Hour + Alice show (if skipped Saturday)
- PM: **Pizza Olympics indoor room** + Carnegie International if not done Saturday
- EVE: Hidden Harbor + Cure
- This is roughly the same plan as Saturday duplicated — not ideal, but everything's open both days.

### Sunday-replacement option (if you'd rather not gamble on Sunday weather)
Move the headlines to **Saturday** and use Sunday as a rest day:
- Sat AM/PM: Phipps Alice + Strip District Market + Carnegie International (already planned)
- **Sat 4 PM: add Pizza Olympics** — wait, that's Sunday-only. So instead:
- Sat 6 PM: MCG Jazz · Tamara Tunie (planned)
- **Sun**: Sleep in, do **Row House Cinema** + **Hidden Harbor** + a quiet **Mattress Factory** visit. Skip the high-stakes outdoor events entirely.
