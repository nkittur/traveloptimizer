The recommendation

Midea Cube 20-Pint (MAD20S1QWT) ~$199

The knee of the price-vs-performance curve across 53 models: the quietest unit Consumer Reports has ever tested, ENERGY STAR Most Efficient, gravity-hose drain (no pump), and cheap. For a mildly-damp basement, 20-pint is enough — and the small, pumpless Cube is the reliable tier of the family.

Runner-up — hOmeLabs 35-Pint (4,500 sq ft class) (~$219.99): The Reddit reliability champion — owners routinely report 5+ trouble-free years. ~$20 more, louder, less efficient, so it lands just off the frontier. Buy it if a proven multi-year track record matters more to you than quiet.
More headroom: If the basement turns out damper than "mild," the 35-pint Cube is the same whisper-quiet/efficient platform (CR's highest-rated model), +$30 for capacity margin.
Avoid: Any Midea 50-pint WITH built-in pump (confirmed early-death pattern, and you don't need a pump), and the swarm of no-name Amazon brands (AEOCKY, Landeagle, FreAire, VEVOR, Shinco, etc.) — cheap, but 5.0★ on a handful of reviews with no reliability track record (L10).
The tradeoff — Quiet + efficiency (Midea Cube) vs. proven longevity (hOmeLabs) vs. lowest price (Midea 30 traditional). The pick leans Cube 20 because your stated must ("not too noisy"), the cheaper-is-better steer, and the energy weight all favor it, and the reliability risk is mitigated by going small + pumpless.
Conviction: reached. Widened to 53 models across 6 Amazon sweeps + 4 editorial sources; 10 shortlisted onto the curve. Triangulated via CR (lab + reliability survey + measured dB), DehumidifierBuyersGuide (measured dB/IEF), Amazon (live prices + critical-review histograms), and Reddit (multi-year longevity). The wider net confirmed the pick and surfaced a real budget-frontier alternative (Midea 30-pint traditional, $178).
