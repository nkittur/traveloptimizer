California Leg

60-72°F · Perfect — never hot, foggy mornings burn off by noon

Santa Cruz (3-4 nights)

  • Visit James (rare — he's only there until August)
  • Beach Boardwalk — free entry, rides, Friday night movies on the beach
  • Henry Cowell Redwoods — easy hiking 15 min from town
  • Downtown boba scene for Ashi, indie coffee shops
  • Capitola Village (10 min south) — colorful beachfront cottages, boutiques
  • Natural Bridges State Beach — tide pools, rock arch

Monterey / Carmel Day Trip

  • Monterey Bay Aquarium — world-class
  • Crystal Fish — best sushi on the peninsula
  • Akaoni — locals' pick for best sushi
  • Carmel-by-the-Sea — fairy-tale downtown, boutiques, white sand beach
  • Point Lobos — stunning coastal reserve, easy walks
  • Cannery Row — historic waterfront
45 min drive on scenic Highway 1. Not SF-level Japanese food, but Crystal Fish and Akaoni are genuinely good. Seafood is excellent — fresh from Monterey Bay.

Door County Leg

70-80°F · 3-4 nights around Aug 7 · Wisconsin's Cape Cod

Supper Clubs (Carissa's Dream)

  • Nightingale Sturgeon Bay, since 1913 — steaks, prime rib, old-fashioneds
  • The Mill Since 1930 — German + American, schnitzel to steaks
  • Sister Bay Bowl Since 1958 — "Best Fish Fry" & "Best Old Fashioned" in Door County
  • The English Inn Beef wellington, garden patio, former lumber mill
  • The Log Den Egg Harbor — hand-carved log building, five generations

Must-Do

  • Fish boil — dramatic spectator cooking event, unique to Door County
  • Cana Island Lighthouse
  • Washington Island ferry → Rock Island
  • Kayaking along the coast
  • Cherry everything (Door County's signature)
Not a sushi destination — this leg is about supper clubs, fish boils, and Wisconsin comfort. California covers the foodie hits; Door County covers the vibe.
